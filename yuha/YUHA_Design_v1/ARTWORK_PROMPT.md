## 最终提示词

Design a beautifully art-directed brand launch poster for YUHA, a youth-oriented music creation brand. Use the supplied reference logo as the immutable logo artwork: the same flat vermilion orange asymmetric petal / abstract y icon and the same rounded black lowercase yuha lettering, exact silhouette, wordmark, proportions and spelling. This is a presentation of that exact logo, not a redesign. LANDSCAPE 3:2 composition. Minimal Japanese editorial fashion direction. Warm off-white paper background (#F6F5F0), charcoal (#20221F), petal orange (#F46B45), lots of intentional blank space. Position the logo large in the left-middle with exquisite spacing. Under the logo add exactly the Chinese slogan "让心动，有回声。" in tasteful modern sans Chinese type, crisp readable, no other Chinese. At upper left tiny "YUHA / 羽花 / ユハ", and lower left tiny "MUSIC BEGINS WITH A FEELING." Treat that English line as a small contextual caption. On the right, one huge sculptural folded orange petal suspended in air, inspired by the outline of the logo, elegant paper-thin material with a delicate cast shadow showing its lift, curved not botanical realistic; controlled gallery lighting, beautifully crafted tactile object but not a cosmetic advertising pack. Bottom edge a very restrained strip showing one small monochrome version of the same icon, one orange rounded-square app icon with charcoal same petal, and three color swatches, visually aligned. No music notes, no headphones, no equalizer, no waveform, no butterflies, no extra brands, no gradients inside the logo, no shiny chrome, no purple neon, no fake product claims, no watermarks. The logo must remain flat vector clean and be the hero; the sculptural petal is supporting imagery. Aim for a confident contemporary record-label-meets-lifestyle identity, bold enough for a young creator community, not a feminine skincare identity.
